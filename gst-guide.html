GST explained: understand price before and after tax

GST calculator use karne se pehle, inclusive aur exclusive price ka simple difference samjho.

Quick answer: GST is a tax framework applied to many supplies of goods and services in India. The rate and treatment can vary by item, service, location and rules. A calculator can help with arithmetic, but it cannot determine the correct tax treatment for every transaction.

Inclusive vs. exclusive price

GST exclusive means the displayed base price does not yet include GST. The applicable GST amount is added to calculate the final invoice total.

GST inclusive means the displayed price already includes GST. In that case, the calculator works backward to estimate the base value and the GST component.

Simple math example

For an exclusive base amount of ₹1,000 at an assumed 18% rate, the GST amount is ₹180 and the total becomes ₹1,180. This is only an arithmetic example. It does not mean 18% applies to every item or service.

Use a calculator carefully

  • First select whether your number is tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive.
  • Enter a rate only after confirming it applies to the particular supply.
  • Keep invoice records and calculate rounding consistently.
  • Do not use a basic calculator as a substitute for compliance, return filing, registration checks or professional advice.
Important: GST rules, rates, exemptions, registration requirements and invoice rules can change. For a business transaction, verify the current official guidance or consult a qualified tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

Does every product have the same GST rate?

No. Rates and treatment can differ. Never assume a rate from one product or service applies to another.

Can this calculator file GST returns?

No. It performs a basic numerical calculation only. Filing and compliance require correct records and current regulatory information.

Bottom line

A GST calculator is useful for checking simple price calculations. It is not a tax decision tool. Verify the correct rate and treatment before issuing or paying an invoice.
